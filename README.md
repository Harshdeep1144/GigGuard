# GigGuard: AI Parametric Income Protection

## Project Overview
GigGuard is an AI-powered parametric insurance platform that protects gig worker’s income by automatically detecting disruptions and delivering instant payouts through a flexible weekly subscription model.

## User Persona
The platform caters to **"The Resilient Independent"**:

- **Profile:** Full-time or high-volume gig workers (Uber, DoorDash, Upwork)
- **Pain Point:** A single day of car trouble, illness, or platform downtime results in zero income
- **Need:** Low-friction, affordable coverage that feels like a subscription rather than a complex legal contract

## Workflow

1. **Onboarding:** User links their work platforms (e.g., Uber API) and bank account  
2. **Monitoring:** The AI engine monitors real-time activity levels against historical benchmarks  
3. **Trigger Event:** If activity drops by 80% due to a covered event, a disruption is flagged  
4. **Instant Payout:** Funds are pushed to the user's digital wallet within minutes  

## Weekly Packages

### Starter (Essential)
Covers platform-wide technical outages and extreme weather disruptions.

### Pro (Balanced)
Includes Starter + coverage for minor vehicle breakdowns or short-term illness.

### Max (Total Protection)
Includes Pro + protection against sudden deactivation or extended medical leave.

## AI Usage

- **Anomaly Detection:** Detects involuntary income disruption
- **External Data Correlation:** Uses weather, traffic, and platform status APIs
- **Predictive Risk Modeling:** Adjusts risk scores based on geography and history

## Weekly Pricing Model

GigGuard uses a **Dynamic Weekly Subscription**:

- Premiums deducted every Monday
- Users can pause coverage anytime

**Pricing Formula:**

Premium = (Base Rate × Risk Factor) + Platform Fees

## Fraud Detection

The AI analyzes:

- Multi-app syncing
- GPS verification
- Behavioral pattern recognition
