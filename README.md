📖 Overview

GigFund is a parametric insurance platform built for workers who don't have a traditional safety net. By leveraging AI to monitor platform data and environmental triggers, we eliminate the friction of traditional insurance.

No claims. No adjusters. No waiting. If your income stops due to factors outside your control, your payout starts.

👤 The Persona: "The Modern Independent"

GigFund is built for Rideshare Drivers, Delivery Couriers, and Freelance Contractors who face:

Income Fragility: One day of downtime = missing a bill payment.

Systemic Risk: App outages or regional events that stop all work.

Bureaucracy: Traditional insurance is too slow and requires too much paperwork.

🔄 How It Works (The Workflow)

Our platform operates on a "Parametric" model—payouts are triggered by pre-defined data points, not by manual inspections.

🔗 Smart Sync: Connect your Uber, DoorDash, or Upwork accounts via secure APIs.

🧠 AI Baseling: Our models learn your average earning patterns to define what "normal" looks like.

📡 Live Monitoring: We track regional app status, weather, and traffic data 24/7.

⚡ Instant Trigger: If the AI detects a 30%+ drop in regional platform activity or a verified health event, it triggers.

💰 Auto-Payout: Funds are deposited into your linked wallet within minutes.

🤖 AI Logic & Integrity

📊 Dynamic Premium Calculation

Our AI analyzes your Earning Volatility. Drivers with consistent schedules get lower rates, while those in high-risk zones or unstable markets receive tailored suggestions to ensure they are adequately protected.

🛡️ Fraud Prevention

To ensure the fund remains sustainable, our AI cross-references:

GPS Telemetry: Ensures the worker was actually "available" for work during the disruption.

Global Event Correlation: Matches individual income drops against wider platform outages.

🔮 Proactive Underwriting

Our system doesn't wait for you to tell us there is a problem. The AI scans platform status pages and news feeds to identify "Mass Trigger Events" (like a city-wide server crash) and prepares payouts for all affected users simultaneously.

🛠 Platform Features

Personal Safety Score: A real-time metric showing your current coverage health.

Flex-Pay: Scale your coverage up or down every Sunday night.

Instant Wallet: Move your payout to your bank via Stripe or Crypto rails immediately.
