# GigGuard – AI-Driven Parametric Insurance Platform

## 👤 Persona

**Primary Users:**

* Gig workers (e.g., delivery partners, ride-share drivers, freelancers)
* Individuals with irregular income streams dependent on external conditions

**User Characteristics:**

* Income instability due to platform dependency
* Limited or no access to traditional insurance
* High sensitivity to disruptions like weather, app outages, or demand drops
* Need for fast, transparent, and reliable financial protection

---

## ⚠️ Problem Statement

Gig workers face unpredictable income disruptions caused by factors beyond their control, such as:

* Platform outages
* Extreme weather conditions
* Sudden drop in demand
* Regulatory restrictions or strikes

Traditional insurance models:

* Require lengthy claims processes
* Lack transparency
* Are not tailored for short-term, dynamic income risks

**Problem:**
How can we provide a fast, automated, and fair insurance solution that protects gig workers’ income in real time without requiring manual claims?

---

## 🔄 Workflow

1. **User Onboarding**

   * User signs up and links their work platform (e.g., ride-sharing or delivery app)
   * Basic profile and income data are collected

2. **Risk Assessment**

   * AI models analyze historical earnings, location, and external risk factors

3. **Policy Selection**

   * User selects a weekly insurance plan based on predicted income risk

4. **Continuous Monitoring**

   * System monitors real-time data (weather, platform status, demand signals)

5. **Disruption Detection**

   * AI detects anomalies or disruptions impacting earnings

6. **Automatic Payout**

   * If predefined conditions are met, payout is triggered instantly
   * No manual claim required

---

## 🤖 AI Usage

AI is central to the platform and is used in the following ways:

### 1. Risk Prediction

* Predicts likelihood of income disruption using:

  * Historical earnings data
  * Weather patterns
  * Location-based demand trends

### 2. Disruption Detection

* Real-time anomaly detection models identify:

  * Sudden drop in earnings
  * Platform outages
  * External disruptions

### 3. Premium Optimization

* Dynamically adjusts weekly pricing based on:

  * Risk profile
  * User behavior
  * Environmental factors

### 4. Fraud Detection

* Identifies suspicious patterns such as:

  * Intentional inactivity
  * Manipulated location or activity data

---

## 💸 Weekly Pricing Model

Unlike traditional insurance, GigGuard uses a **flexible weekly pricing model**:

* Users subscribe on a **weekly basis**
* Premium is calculated dynamically based on:

  * Predicted income
  * Risk exposure
  * Historical consistency

### Key Features:

* No long-term commitment
* Pay only for the weeks you work
* Transparent pricing breakdown
* Adaptive pricing (changes with risk level)

---

## 🧩 Platform Usage

### 🔢 Premium Calculation

Premium is calculated using:

* Average weekly income
* Risk probability score (from AI models)
* External risk factors (weather, demand, location)

**Example Formula:**

```
Premium = (Expected Weekly Income × Risk Score × Adjustment Factor)
```

The system ensures fairness through:

* Triggered automatically when disruption conditions are met

## 🚀 Summary

GigGuard redefines insurance for the gig economy by:

* Eliminating manual claims
* Using AI for real-time decision-making
* Offering flexible, weekly pricing
* Ensuring fast and transparent payouts

It empowers gig workers with financial stability in an unpredictable work environment.
